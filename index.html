About Tekton

A premier manufacturer of state-of-the-art vertical transportation.

Backed by over two decades of engineering mastery, we deliver custom-designed elevators and escalators engineered for absolute safety, energy efficiency and smooth aesthetic integration into modern architecture.

  • 20+ years of engineering mastery across residential, commercial and industrial projects
  • 300+ successful installations commissioned and maintained
  • 100% compliance with IS 14665 electric traction lift safety standards
  • 24/7 emergency rescue with Auto Rescue Devices fitted as standard

Our mission

To deliver next-generation vertical transportation systems powered by smart IoT telemetry and AI predictive maintenance. By prioritising engineering precision, strict safety standards and real-time remote monitoring, we guarantee maximum reliability and total client peace of mind.

Our vision

To become the global leader in AI-driven, IoT-integrated vertical mobility — delivering world-class technology, zero-downtime reliability and uncompromised safety at accessible, market-leading value.

Lifecycle engineering & smart technology

Intelligence built into every shaft.

From the first CAD line to the tenth year of service, your lift stays measured, monitored and predictable.

01

In-house custom CAD design

Custom 2D and 3D shaft and cabin tailoring for exact site conditions — pit depth, headroom and architectural constraints resolved before fabrication.

02

Precision installation & IS compliance

Government-licensed execution strictly under IS 14665-1 (2000) electric traction lift safety standards, with Auto Rescue Devices fitted as standard.

03

IoT-connected remote telemetry

Real-time 24/7 cloud monitoring of motor temperature, door cycles, weight sensors and voltage levels for proactive system health management.

04

AI predictive maintenance & AMC

Machine-learning models that flag component wear before failure occurs, so annual maintenance contracts protect uptime instead of chasing breakdowns.

Scope of service

Complete lifecycle support, one accountable partner.

  1. Consultation & site assessment

    Advanced site survey, traffic analysis and capacity planning, with optimised architectural blueprints for your building.

  2. 3D design & approval

    Site-customised shaft layouts, pit dimensions and cabin interiors presented in 2D drawings and 3D visualisation before a single part is cut.
